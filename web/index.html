Local connection

Pair your laptop.

Enter the six-digit code shown in the WinRemote window on your Windows laptop. Connect using the same Wi-Fi network, or use Bluetooth PAN when supported by your phone.

Use Bluetooth instead of Wi-Fi
  1. On your Android phone, enable Bluetooth tethering in Hotspot and tethering settings.
  2. Pair the phone and laptop in Windows Bluetooth settings.
  3. On Windows, connect to the phone's Personal Area Network (PAN). The exact menu depends on your Bluetooth driver.
  4. Restart WinRemote and open the address labelled Bluetooth PAN in its console, then enter the pairing code.

This is Bluetooth networking (PAN), not Bluetooth HID. Some phones do not allow the phone browser to reach a laptop connected as a PAN client. When that happens, use Wi-Fi. Native Bluetooth keyboard/mouse pairing requires a separate mobile app and is not provided by this browser build.

Your connection stays on a local network. Never share your pairing code or expose this app to the internet.

Lock your laptop
Immediately lock your Windows session.
WinRemote can lock Windows, but it cannot enter your PIN on the sign-in screen. Windows protects credential screens from remote keystroke injection. Unlock locally, or use a supported remote-desktop sign-in setup. Never send your Windows PIN through this page.
